NEW SaaS: DAY 1 EXPERIENCE

Transform bloated on-prem product to user-friendly SaaS

Product story

Role

Manager

Objective

Deliver a SaaS-based networking tool that is so easy a user can “try it on their lunch break”. Users should be able to go from account creation through device discovery, guided by out-of-the-box reports that build familiarity with the tool fast.

Overview

Summary of project

SevOne SaaS is a cloud-based network observability tool that provides customers with faster onboarding and time to insights, enhanced network team productivity and efficiency, flexible, open pricing model to reduce upfront costs, a single source of truth in a centralized tool, and automatic updates with centralized maintenance.

Problem

Organizations face significant challenges in managing their growing network complexity including:

  • Rising CapEx investments
  • Need to scale with growing network footprint
  • Lack of single pane of glass experience
  • Longer time to resolution
  • Tedious upgrades and updates
  • Challenges of a global, remote workforce

Additionally, the current on-prem solution for SevOne can be overly complex for many smaller companies and the onboarding process can take weeks or months and often requires the assistance of expert services.

Process

(9 months)

Key activities

DISCOVERY + LOWFI EXPLORATION

  • Re-organized larger design team to continue covering existing projects while assigning best team for this project
  • Aligned with PM on requirements, scope, and timeline
  • Understand on-prem as-is experience
  • Conduct competitive analysis
  • Collaborated with architecture and engineering to understand technical feasibility and approach
  • Ideated on solutions in mural and figma

MIDFI + HIFI DESIGNS

  • Design a solution that reduces onboarding time from up to 3 months down to 30 minutes
  • Design an out of the box overview dashboard for quicker insights
  • Enhance many on-prem features including flow explorer, filters, and policy creation.
  • Design new synthetics capability
  • Design new AI capability
  • Design multiple integration use cases for larger cross-IBM IT platform

SUMMARY

RESULTS

  • Reduced onboarding time from up to 3 months down to 30 minutes
  • Simplified user experiences to promote PLG-led sales activities
  • Provided a SaaS-based networking observability solution for the IBM Concert Platform strategy
  • Enabled SaaS-based integration opportunities such as internet weathermaps, "Is it the app or the network" integration with Instana, and enhanced AI insights with IBM Network Intelligence
